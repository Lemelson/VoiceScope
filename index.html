Analysis settings
Changing anything below switches the profile to Custom — your own preset.
Outlier trimming Percentile trimming removes the given % of the lowest and highest values across the whole recording. "Smart" looks for short glitches against neighbouring frames and also cuts very short global extremes near pauses; sustained pitch changes are kept.
Sensitivity 0.55 Confidence threshold of the pitch detector. Lower catches more quiet/unclear sounds (and noise). Higher keeps only clear, stable tone. If your voice gets lost — lower it; if it picks up junk — raise it.
MA window 72 ms How much time the moving average smooths over. The window is centred on each point.
EMA span 72 ms Equivalent span of the exponential moving average. Smaller reacts faster; larger smooths more.
Fixes spurious jumps of exactly 2× up/down when the detector confuses the octave. Makes the contour smoother.
Min duration 3 Drops voiced bursts shorter than N frames — usually clicks, breaths and artifacts, not voice. 0 — keep all.
Estimates the background noise level from quiet sections and cuts anything below it. Helps in a noisy room.

Online voice pitch analyzer

VoiceScope measures the fundamental frequency (F0) of your voice, converts it to musical notes, and shows how your pitch changed over time — useful for comparing recordings, watching intonation, and finding your comfortable speaking range. It's a clean, private alternative to voicecel.

Recording and analysis happen locally with the YIN autocorrelation algorithm; nothing is uploaded. Results are a reference, not a medical assessment.
